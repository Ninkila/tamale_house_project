About Tamale House Market

Tamale House Market is a real estate marketplace dedicated to connecting landlords listing residential rental properties with prospective tenants seeking accommodation in Tamale, Ghana.

Our Mission

Searching for rental accommodation across Tamale can often be time-consuming and fragmented. Tamale House Market offers a centralized, accessible digital platform where property details, rental budgets, and direct contact options (WhatsApp & Direct Phone) are transparently available to everyone.

Platform Standards

  • Verified pricing formatted transparently in Ghana Cedis (GH₵).
  • Accurate suburb and neighbourhood categorization (including *Lamashegu*, *Nyohini*, *Dungu*, *Vittin*, *Fuo*, *Tishegu*, *Education Ridge*, and more).
  • Direct landlord communication via standard international WhatsApp links and phone dialers.
  • Dedicated landlord and tenant dashboards for managing listings and saved favourite properties.
